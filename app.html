Report definitions
Metrics are calculated from editable sample records and can be replaced by database queries later.
Reorder point = lead-time demand + safety stock. EOQ = √(2 × annual demand × ordering cost ÷ annual holding cost per unit). Supplier on-time = received POs on/before expected date ÷ received POs. Fill rate = received quantity ÷ ordered quantity. Production yield = good units ÷ started units.
